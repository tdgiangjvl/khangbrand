Privacy Policy

"What If Li YouTube" · last updated November 14, 2026

"What If Li YouTube" (the "app") is a private tool used only by the owner of the What If Li YouTube channels to manage those channels. This policy explains what the app accesses and what it does with it.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What the app accesses

  • Channel information (channel id, title, handle, public statistics) of the channel you choose when signing in, to confirm which channel is connected.
  • Videos you upload through the app and their metadata (title, description, tags, thumbnail, playlist, publish time), to upload and schedule them.
  • YouTube Analytics reports of your own channel (views, watch time, retention), to plan future videos.
  • Public data such as the most popular videos in a country, read with an API key.

The app does not access viewers' personal data, comments' authors' accounts, payments, or any channel other than the ones you authorise.

How it is stored

OAuth refresh tokens and analytics reports are stored only on the owner's own computer, in files readable by that user alone. Nothing is stored on a server run by the app. Data is kept only as long as needed to run the channels; analytics reports older than 30 days are refreshed or deleted, as the YouTube API Services policies require.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer any data obtained through YouTube API Services to anyone. No advertising or tracking is used.

Revoking access

You can remove the app's access at any time on the Google security settings page. Local tokens are then useless and are deleted.

Changes

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions: contact the owner through the channel's About page.
