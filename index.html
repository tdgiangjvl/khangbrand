What If Li

Lì lives in parallel universes where exactly one rule of our world is different. Each episode follows Lì through one of them, then comes back to ours with real precedents, studies and numbers, every one of them sourced.

The channels are made in Vietnamese first (Lì ở vũ trụ song song), then in English, Japanese, Thai and Indonesian.

The "What If Li YouTube" app

"What If Li YouTube" is a private tool used only by the channels' owner. It signs in to the owner's own YouTube channels through Google OAuth to upload and schedule the channels' videos, set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and playlists, and read the channels' own analytics. It is not offered to anyone else.
